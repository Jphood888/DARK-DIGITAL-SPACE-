Questions
Good to know
How do I get a DDS Number?

Tap Get your DDS Number, verify your email with the 6-digit code and send your application. When it is approved, your DDS Number is emailed to you. You then use it to register your account.

Is Dead Drop anonymous?

Drops don't show who posted them, and replies and reactions reach the owner as “Anonymous”. Drops can only be viewed inside DDS, they can't be downloaded, and they disappear after enough views or 24 hours.

Where do I get DDS Codelab?

When it goes live, a download bar appears at the top of this page and the Codelab card below switches to Download APK. It's for Android. If your phone asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser.

What happens to my data?

We keep only what's needed to run your account. Read the details in our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

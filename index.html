// what is dds

A place for
digital innovators & builders.

DDS — Dark Digital Space — is a specialized digital forum for global gatherings of individuals with interests in cybersecurity, hacking, and programming 🌐. It operates under the premise of sharing knowledge, skills, and entertainment related to these fields, while emphasizing ethical conduct and security 🔒.

The platform facilitates educational and recreational interactions among its members, fostering a community that values learning, innovation, and responsible engagement with technology 💡. To start to be part of this great system, get a dds number. See it as a phone number but in dds system. This maintains the whole concept of identification of a user in dds. What to do is to apply for one using your email and phone number e.g, +234, +1 etc... after you send you application, your dds number application gets verified by admins and approved in between 24-48hour. After they get your application approved you get your dds number sent to the email you used to apply, no stress. After you get your DDS number you get to register with your dds number. Remember to add the brackets to your dds number because it's also part of your dds number! After doing that fill out the spaces in the sign-up section and sign up then login to get your self in the system. Get a DDS Number, signup and stay patient till when the time for launch is completed so you can start using the tools and apps dds team provides on the site, e.g, dds codelab, dds app, dds watch crime, dds lock d box etc...

1. Introduction

Dark Digital Space ("DDS", "we", "us" or "our") is an online community for people who are interested in cybersecurity, hacking, programming and building digital projects. We run the website at darkdigitalspace.com.ng and the apps connected to it, including DDS Codelab. This Privacy Policy applies to all of them.

We take privacy seriously because many of our members come to DDS to learn about security and expect us to behave responsibly. We aim to collect only what we need, to keep it safe, to be open about what we do with it and to give you real control wherever we can.

This policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and our Usage Policy.

1.1 Who is responsible for your information

DDS is operated by the DDS team in Nigeria. For the purposes of data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the DDS team is the "data controller" of the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at darkdigitalspaceteam@gmail.com.

1.2 What this policy covers

It covers information that we collect when you visit the website, apply for a DDS Number, register and use an account, post projects, use Dead Drop, take part in challenges and referrals, contact us or use our apps. It does not cover other websites or services that we link to. Those have their own policies.

2. Summary in plain words

This short summary is only a guide. The sections below give the full picture.

  • We collect your email, WhatsApp number, country, alias and profession so that we can run the application and your account.
  • We store your password only in a scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read it.
  • We record technical information such as your IP address and a device identifier to stop abuse, spam and people who make many accounts.
  • Dead Drops are anonymous to other users, but our systems know which account made them so that we can keep people safe.
  • We keep your sign-in and some preferences in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not show you third-party advertising.
  • Companies that help us run DDS, such as Google, GitHub and Vercel, handle some information for us under their own terms.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and you can complain to the regulator.

3. Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us, what our systems record while you use DDS, and what we receive from other services you choose to use with DDS.

3.1 Information you give us

  • When you apply. Your email address, a one-time code that you enter to confirm that you control the address, your WhatsApp number and your country.
  • When you register. Your DDS Number, your chosen alias, your profession or interest, and your password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Your profile. Details that you add, such as a profile picture, a short description, and the links you choose to show.
  • Your Content. Projects, files, images, videos, captions, comments, replies, reactions, Dead Drops, ratings and anything else you post or send.
  • Messages to us. Feedback, support requests, appeals, reports about other users and the details you include in them.
  • Payments and withdrawals. If you buy a bits package or request a withdrawal where it is available, we collect the details needed to process it, such as the amount, the date, a payment reference and the payout details you provide. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Referrals. Your referral code, and the codes that people use when they join through you.

3.2 Information recorded while you use DDS

  • Device and network data. Your IP address, a device identifier (sometimes called a fingerprint), the type of browser and device, the language setting and the approximate time zone.
  • Activity data. Pages and features that you use, the projects you open, the drops you view, the actions you take, the time of each action and your bits, rank, challenge and referral history.
  • Technical logs. Error reports and server logs that help us keep the Service secure and working.
  • Safety signals. Reports from other users about you, results of automated checks for prohibited text, and records of warnings, restrictions, bans and appeals.

3.3 Information from other services

  • Google sign-in. If we offer it and you choose it, Google tells us your email address, whether it is verified, your name and a unique Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Hosting services. Our hosting and storage providers give us technical records such as error logs and delivery data.

3.4 Information we do not want

Please do not give us government identity numbers, health information, bank card numbers or passwords to other services. Our systems are not designed to hold them and we do not ask for them.

4. How we use your information

We use your information for the following purposes.

  • To run the application process and your account. Verifying your email, reviewing Applications, issuing DDS Numbers, creating accounts, signing you in and helping you recover access.
  • To provide the Service. Showing your projects and Dead Drops to the right people, counting views, delivering replies and reactions to the owner, running the bits, rank, boost, challenge and referral systems, and processing purchases and withdrawals.
  • To communicate with you. Sending one-time codes, security alerts, notices about your account, replies to your messages and important announcements about DDS or changes to our policies.
  • To keep DDS safe. Detecting spam and abuse, limiting the number of accounts on a device, enforcing the Usage Policy, investigating reports, preventing fraud, and protecting people from harm.
  • To improve DDS. Understanding which features are used, finding and fixing bugs, and planning new features. Where we can, we use combined or summarised data for this.
  • To meet legal duties. Keeping records that the law requires, answering lawful requests from authorities, and protecting our rights.

We do not use your information to make decisions that have legal effects on you through automated means alone. Automated checks can flag content or behaviour, but we aim to have a person review serious actions, and you can appeal.

5. Why we are allowed to use it (legal bases)

Data protection law requires us to have a valid reason for using personal information. We rely on the following.

  • Contract. We need your information to give you an account and the features you ask for.
  • Consent. For optional things, such as signing in with Google, we ask for your permission. You can withdraw consent at any time, and this will not affect what we did before.
  • Legitimate interests. We have a real interest in keeping DDS secure, preventing abuse and improving the service, and we balance that against your rights.
  • Legal obligation. Sometimes the law requires us to keep or share information.
  • Vital interests. In rare cases we may need to act to protect someone's life or safety.

6. Dead Drop, anonymity and your information

Dead Drop deserves its own explanation because people expect it to be private.

6.1 What other users can see

Other users cannot see who posted a drop. A person who views a drop is not shown to the owner by name. The owner sees a count of views and the anonymous reactions and replies, which appear as "Anonymous" with a number.

6.2 What our systems know

To make drops work, our systems link each drop to the account that created it and record views, reactions and replies against an identifier. For visitors who are not signed in, the identifier is a random key stored in your browser so that the same person is not counted twice and so that reply limits can be applied. This key does not contain your name.

6.3 Why we keep that link

We keep it so that you can manage your own drops, so that we can apply limits fairly, and so that we can act when a drop is used to threaten, harass, expose or exploit people or to share illegal material. We may look at it when we investigate a report, and we may share it with the police or a court where the law requires or where there is a serious risk to someone's safety.

6.4 When a drop ends

A drop stops being available when it reaches its view limit or after twenty-four hours. After that, it is no longer shown in DDS. We may keep limited records of it, such as its identifier, its dates and moderation records, for safety, legal and technical reasons, and copies may remain in backups for a short time.

6.5 Viewing only

Drops are designed to be viewed inside DDS rather than downloaded. This protects the person who posted. We ask everyone not to try to get around that.

7. Local storage, cookies and similar tools

We use your browser's local storage, and similar technology, to make DDS work. These are small pieces of data kept on your device. Examples include:

  • keeping you signed in, by saving the details of your session on your device;
  • remembering a random viewer key for Dead Drop, as described above;
  • remembering small preferences, such as how you chose to view drops;
  • remembering that you have already seen an announcement, so that we do not show it again straight away; and
  • helping the site load faster.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not run third-party advertising trackers on DDS. You can clear this data in your browser settings at any time, but if you do, you will be signed out and some preferences will reset. The sign-in services of other companies, if you choose them, may use their own cookies.

8. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in the following situations.

8.1 Service providers

Some companies help us run DDS and process information for us, under their own terms and only for that purpose.

  • Google provides the spreadsheet-based systems where we store our data, the tools that run our server code, the email service that sends you codes and notices, and, if you choose it, Google sign-in.
  • GitHub hosts some of the images and media files that are uploaded to DDS and some of our site files.
  • Vercel hosts the website and delivers its pages.
  • Payment and payout partners, where we offer purchases or withdrawals, handle the money side of those actions.

8.2 Other users

What you choose to make public, such as your alias, your profile, your projects and your public comments, can be seen by others. Please think before you post. Dead Drops follow the rules in section 6.

8.3 Our team

Only a small number of DDS team members and moderators can reach personal information, and only when their work needs it.

8.4 Legal and safety reasons

We may share information if we believe in good faith that the law requires it, for example because of a court order, or to protect the rights, property or safety of DDS, our users or the public, to investigate fraud or serious abuse, or to respond to a valid request from a public authority.

8.5 Business changes

If DDS is reorganised, merged or transferred, information may be passed to the new owners, who must respect this policy or give you notice before anything changes.

8.6 With your agreement

We may share information in other ways if you ask us to or give your consent.

9. Transfers outside Nigeria

Our service providers operate servers in several countries, so your information may be processed outside Nigeria. When this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure that your information is protected, for example by choosing providers that apply strong security standards and by sharing no more than is needed. If the law requires extra safeguards for a transfer, we will put them in place.

10. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as we need it for the reasons in this policy.

  • Application details are kept while the Application is open and, for approved applicants, for as long as the Account exists.
  • Account details are kept while your Account is active. If you ask us to delete the Account, we remove or anonymise the information that we do not need to keep, usually within thirty days, subject to the exceptions below.
  • One-time codes expire quickly and are cleared after use or expiry.
  • Content stays until you delete it or the Account is closed. Dead Drops follow section 6.4.
  • Safety and fraud records, such as device bans, moderation actions, appeals and reports, may be kept for longer so that we can stop banned people from returning and protect others.
  • Financial records about purchases and withdrawals may be kept for as long as the law or good accounting practice requires.
  • Backups are cleared on a regular cycle, so deleted information may take a little longer to disappear from them.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

11. How we protect your information

We use sensible technical and organisational measures to protect your information. These include:

  • storing passwords in a hashed form so that they are not readable;
  • using encrypted connections (HTTPS) between your device and our website;
  • limiting who can access our data and recording important actions;
  • rate limits and automated checks that help to stop guessing of codes and abuse;
  • reviewing and improving our code and systems on a regular basis; and
  • separating public information from private information.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that your information will never be accessed without permission. You can help by using a strong, unique password, by keeping your devices up to date and by never sharing your DDS Number, password or one-time codes. If we learn of a breach that is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Automated moderation and device checks

To protect the community we use some automated tools.

  • Prohibited-content checks. Text that is posted on DDS may be scanned for phrases linked to serious harm, such as threats, extortion, scams, doxxing, hate speech and sexual content involving minors. A match can lead to review, a restriction or a ban, depending on the seriousness.
  • Device limits. We limit how many accounts can be created from one device. To do this we store a device identifier and associate it with the aliases that use it.
  • Community reports. Reports from other users are recorded and can lead to action when there is a pattern.

Automated tools can be wrong. If you think a decision was a mistake, please use the appeal option described in the Terms & Conditions. A person will review it.

13. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your information is used, which is the purpose of this policy.
  • Access the personal information that we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your information, in many cases.
  • Restrict or object to some kinds of use, including use that is based on our legitimate interests.
  • Data portability, meaning to receive some of your information in a common format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it.
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or to the authority in the country where you live.

13.1 How to use your rights

Please email darkdigitalspaceteam@gmail.com from the address linked to your Account and include your DDS Number and what you would like us to do. We may need to confirm that you are the right person before we act, to protect your information from someone pretending to be you. We aim to reply within thirty days. We will not charge a fee unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

13.2 Limits

Sometimes we cannot do everything you ask. For example, we may need to keep records for safety or legal reasons, or deleting something might affect other people's rights. When this happens, we will explain why.

14. Children

DDS is not for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under 18, we expect that a parent or guardian knows that you use DDS. If we find out that we have collected information from a child under 16, we will delete it and close the Account. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us so that we can act.

15. Links to other websites

DDS may contain links to other websites, apps or files that are shared by users or by us. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies before you give them any information. Files and apps shared by other members may collect information about you when you use them, so please be careful.

16. Your choices and controls

There are several things that you can do yourself.

  • Edit your profile and delete your Content inside the app.
  • Sign out at any time, which removes your session from that device.
  • Clear local storage in your browser to remove saved settings and the anonymous viewer key.
  • Choose how you sign in, and stop using Google sign-in if you wish, by asking us to unlink it.
  • Control announcements by closing them, which tells us not to show them again for a while.
  • Contact us to use your rights as described in section 13.

17. Messages we send you

We send two kinds of messages. The first are service messages, which are needed for your account to work. These include one-time codes, notices about the progress of your Application, your DDS Number, password reset codes, security alerts, notices about bans or appeals, receipts and withdrawal updates. You cannot opt out of these while you have an account, because they are part of the service.

The second are announcements, such as news about new features, launches of apps like DDS Codelab, challenges and community updates. We usually show these inside DDS, for example as a pop-up that you can close. We may also email or message you on WhatsApp or through our channels. We do not send marketing messages on behalf of other companies, and we do not give your contact details to advertisers. If you do not want announcements, tell us and we will stop sending them to you, apart from service messages.

18. Notes for people outside Nigeria

DDS is run from Nigeria, and Nigerian law applies to it. If you live in another country, such as the United Kingdom, a member state of the European Union or another place with its own data protection law, you may have additional rights under that law, for example the right to complain to your local authority. We will respect the rights that you have under the law that applies to you, so far as we are required to do so. By using DDS you understand that your information will be handled in Nigeria and in the countries where our service providers operate, as explained in section 9. If you do not accept this, please do not use DDS.

We also remind everyone that the information you put in public places on DDS, such as your alias, your profile and public comments, may be copied by other people and may remain visible elsewhere even after you delete it from DDS. We cannot control what other people do with information that you chose to make public.

We may update this policy as DDS grows or as the law changes. Each edition has a version number and a date. If we make an important change, we will tell you through DDS or by email before it takes effect, where reasonably possible. Using DDS after a change means that you accept the updated policy.

1. Purpose and scope

Dark Digital Space ("DDS", "we", "us" or "our") brings together people who are interested in cybersecurity, hacking, programming and creating digital projects. A community like this can only work if its members trust each other and trust the platform. This Usage Policy explains the standards that everyone must follow when they use DDS.

It applies to every part of DDS, including the website, the apps, your profile, Explore, Dead Drop, comments, replies, reactions, messages, challenges, boosts, referrals, the learning section and every way in which you contact us. It applies to what you say, what you upload, what you link to and what you do with your account, whether you are signed in or not.

This policy is part of the agreement between you and DDS, together with the Terms & Conditions and the Privacy Policy. If you break it, we may take the actions described in section 16.

2. Our principles

We built these rules around a few ideas.

  • Learning is welcome. Understanding how systems break is how people learn to defend them. Curiosity is not a crime on DDS.
  • Permission matters. The difference between a researcher and an attacker is usually authorisation. Test only what you own or have clear permission to test.
  • People come first. No project, joke or experiment is worth harming a real person.
  • Honesty keeps us safe. Say what your project does. Do not hide what a file does or who you are.
  • Anonymity is a tool, not a licence. Dead Drop protects privacy, not wrongdoing.
  • Fair play. Bits, ranks and rewards mean something only if everyone earns them honestly.
  • The law applies. DDS members live in many countries. You must follow the laws that apply to you, and Nigerian law applies to DDS itself.

3. Accounts and identity

3.1 One person, one account

Each person may have one account. Do not create extra accounts to get around a ban, a limit, a challenge or a referral rule. We limit the number of accounts that can be created from one device, and we may link accounts that appear to be controlled by the same person.

3.2 Your DDS Number

Your DDS Number is personal. Do not sell, trade, lend, publish or share it. Do not buy or use a number that was issued to someone else.

3.3 Aliases and profiles

Choose an alias that is not offensive, does not copy another person or brand and does not suggest that you speak for DDS. Do not use a profile picture, bio or link that breaks this policy. Impersonating a DDS team member, a moderator, a company or another member is not allowed, even as a joke.

3.4 Keep your account secure

Do not share your password or one-time codes. Do not use another person's account, even if they offer it. Tell us if you think your account has been accessed by someone else.

4. What you may not post or do

The following are not allowed anywhere on DDS. We group them by how serious they are, which is the same scale that we use in moderation (see section 16).

4.1 Most serious: immediate permanent ban

These are the most harmful categories. Posting them can lead to an immediate permanent ban of the account and the device, removal of the content, and a report to the authorities.

  • Sexual content involving minors. Any sexual or sexualised material about a person under 18, in any form, including drawings, stories, chats and links. There are no exceptions.
  • Violent threats. Threatening to kill, hurt, kidnap or attack a person or group, including "jokes" that a reasonable person would take seriously.
  • Terrorism and extremism. Praising, recruiting for, funding or helping violent extremist groups or acts.
  • Hate speech. Attacking people because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar characteristics, including slurs used to demean.
  • Self-harm encouragement. Telling or urging someone to harm themselves or end their life, or promoting suicide or self-harm.
  • Human trafficking. Content that recruits for, advertises or helps trafficking or exploitation of people.

If you are struggling with thoughts of harming yourself, please contact local emergency services or a trusted person. You matter, and you do not have to face it alone.

4.2 Very serious: severe penalties

These can lead to long suspensions or permanent bans, depending on the case and your history.

  • Doxxing and exposing personal information. Sharing, or threatening to share, someone's private details, such as home address, phone number, identity documents, private messages, photos or accounts, without consent. This applies to anonymous drops too.
  • Extortion and blackmail. Demanding money, favours or silence by threatening to expose or harm someone.
  • Selling weapons or drugs. Offering, arranging or advertising the sale of illegal weapons or drugs.
  • Distributing malware and hacking tools made to cause harm. See section 5 for the detailed rules.

4.3 Serious: warnings, removal and restrictions

  • Graphic violence. Detailed or gratuitous descriptions or images of violence meant to shock or glorify harm.
  • Harassment and bullying. Repeatedly targeting a person with insults, threats, unwanted contact, shaming or pile-ons.
  • Scams and phishing. Fake giveaways, fake login pages, links that steal details, "investment" schemes and anything designed to trick people out of money or information.
  • Fraud solicitation. Asking others to take part in fraud, fake documents, carding, money laundering or similar crimes.
  • Animal cruelty. Content that shows or encourages cruelty to animals.

4.4 Moderate: removal, warnings and loss of rewards

  • Impersonation. Pretending to be another person, organisation or DDS staff.
  • Coordinated spam and bot-like behaviour. Posting the same thing repeatedly, using scripts to flood the platform, or coordinating groups to push content.

4.5 Minor: warnings and removal

  • Unsolicited advertising. Promoting products, channels or services that nobody asked about, or posting links only to gain traffic.
  • Cheating and sharing exploits. Sharing methods to cheat in challenges or to exploit bugs in DDS for unfair gain. Report bugs instead.

4.6 Other illegal content

Do not use DDS to share, promote or carry out anything that is illegal where you live or where DDS operates, including pirated content that you do not have the right to share, stolen data and illegal services.

5. Security research, hacking content and tools

DDS exists for people who are curious about security, so this section is important. We want ethical learning and sharing to thrive, and we want to stop real harm.

5.1 What is welcome

  • Writing, tutorials and write-ups about how vulnerabilities and attacks work, in order to teach and to defend.
  • Tools for testing your own systems, practice labs, capture-the-flag challenges and training material.
  • Proof-of-concept code that is clearly labelled, limited and safe to examine, shared for learning.
  • Discussions of responsible disclosure, defensive practice, forensics, reverse engineering of software you are allowed to analyse, and secure coding.
  • Programming projects, games, utilities and apps of every kind.

5.2 What is not allowed

  • Malware and harmful payloads. Ransomware, spyware, stalkerware, keyloggers, remote access trojans used without consent, botnet tools, credential stealers, cryptominers that run without clear consent, and files that include a hidden harmful payload.
  • Attacks on real targets. Using DDS to organise, brag about or carry out attacks on systems, networks, accounts or people without permission. This includes denial-of-service, intrusion, data theft, SIM swapping and account takeover.
  • Stolen data. Posting leaked databases, passwords, tokens, keys, card numbers or private files, or links to them.
  • Targeting lists. Sharing lists of vulnerable systems or accounts in order to help others to attack them.
  • Fraud tooling. Tools whose main purpose is phishing, carding, fake identity creation or evading payment and identity checks.
  • Evading law enforcement. Advice meant to help people commit crimes and avoid being caught.

5.3 The test we use

When we decide whether a post is acceptable, we look at purpose, context, risk and authorisation. Is it clearly educational or defensive? Does it include a plain explanation and safe handling instructions? Could it be used immediately against ordinary people with little skill? Is a real target named? A clear educational frame with responsible detail is usually fine. A ready-to-use weapon aimed at ordinary users is not.

5.4 If you find a vulnerability in DDS

Please tell us privately at darkdigitalspaceteam@gmail.com. Give us a reasonable amount of time to fix it before you share it. Do not access other people's data, do not damage or overload the service, and do not demand payment. If you act in good faith and follow these rules, we will treat your report as a favour and will not take action against you.

6. Projects, tools, scripts and apps in Explore

Anyone may be downloading what you publish, so extra care is needed.

  • Be honest. Give an accurate title and description. Say what the project does, what permissions it needs, what data it touches and where it connects.
  • No hidden behaviour. Do not hide tracking, advertising, data collection, remote control or anything else that a reasonable user would object to.
  • Label risk. If a project is a security tool that could be misused, say so clearly and describe safe and legal use.
  • Own your work. Post only projects that you made or are allowed to share. Respect licences and credit original authors.
  • Keep it clean. Test your files before you upload them. Do not upload files that are infected, even by accident, and remove or fix them quickly if someone reports a problem.
  • Android apps. If you share an APK, explain what permissions it needs and why. Never disguise an app as a different one.
  • No misleading promotion. Do not use fake screenshots, fake ratings, fake download counts or fake reviews.

We may remove projects, mark them with a warning or ask you to change them. If a project is a risk to people, we may remove it at once.

7. Dead Drop rules

Dead Drop gives people a way to share something without putting a name on it. These rules protect everyone who uses it.

  • Anonymity is not impunity. Our systems link every drop to an account. We can and will act on drops that break this policy, and may share information with the authorities where the law requires or where someone is at serious risk.
  • No exposure. Do not use a drop to expose, shame or identify a person, to share their private details or intimate images, or to encourage others to target them.
  • No illegal material. Never post sexual content involving minors, content that shows real violence in order to shock, threats, or any other content that this policy forbids.
  • View only. Do not download, record, scrape or republish another person's drop. Do not try to remove or bypass protections that stop copying. Do not use tools to count views or to inflate view numbers.
  • Respect limits. Do not try to get around reply limits, view limits or the time limit. Do not create extra accounts or guest identities to do so.
  • Unmasking. Do not try to work out who posted a drop or who replied, and do not claim to know. Do not threaten to unmask anyone.
  • Consent. Do not post images or videos of people who do not know about it or would not agree, especially in private places or in moments of distress.

8. Comments, replies, reactions and messages

  • Disagree with ideas, not with people. Keep conversations respectful, even when you strongly disagree.
  • No insults, threats, slurs, harassment or repeated unwanted contact. If someone asks you to stop, stop.
  • Anonymous replies and reactions must follow the same rules as everything else. Anonymity does not change what is acceptable.
  • Do not send unsolicited private messages that advertise, scam or pressure people.
  • Do not share private messages from other people without their permission.
  • Do not post spam, chain messages, fake alerts or deliberately misleading information.

9. Spam, automation and manipulation

  • No unauthorised automation. Do not use bots, scripts, scrapers or automated tools to create accounts, post, view, react, reply, vote, download or collect data on DDS, unless we have given you written permission.
  • No manipulation. Do not manipulate views, ratings, downloads, boosts, rankings or any other metric, through bots, multiple accounts, paid groups or tricks.
  • No flooding. Do not overload the service or send large numbers of requests that harm its performance.
  • No data harvesting. Do not collect other members' personal information from DDS.
  • No resale. Do not sell access to DDS features, accounts, bits or ranks.

10. Bits, ranks, referrals, challenges and boosts

These systems reward taking part. To keep them fair:

  • earn bits only through the actions that DDS provides, and not through exploits, bugs, multiple accounts or fake activity;
  • do not use your own referral code on an account that you control, do not create fake sign-ups, and do not spam or mislead people to get them to join;
  • do not pay, threaten or trick people into joining or entering a challenge;
  • do not rig challenges, collude with other players, or use outside help that the rules forbid;
  • do not try to buy, sell or trade bits or ranks outside the features that DDS provides;
  • do not file false payment or withdrawal claims, and do not use stolen payment methods;
  • report any bug or exploit that you find in the bits or rewards systems to us instead of using it.

We may correct, reverse, freeze or remove bits, ranks and rewards that were obtained unfairly, cancel withdrawals, and restrict or close the accounts involved.

11. Protecting the platform

  • Do not probe, scan, attack, overload or try to break into DDS, its servers, its apps or its data, except as allowed in section 5.4.
  • Do not try to reach accounts, files, drops or data that are not yours.
  • Do not reverse engineer, decompile or modify our apps, except where the law allows.
  • Do not avoid or disable any security feature, limit, ban or device restriction. Creating new accounts or using new devices to return after a ban is a serious violation.
  • Do not post links or files that attack other visitors, such as scripts that run in their browsers.
  • Do not interfere with how the site counts, records or displays information.

12. Intellectual property

  • Post only Content that you own or have a right to use.
  • Respect copyright, trademarks and licences, including open-source licences.
  • Do not share pirated software, cracked apps, license key generators or paid course material without permission.
  • If you believe someone has used your work without permission, follow the complaint process in the Terms & Conditions.

13. Privacy of other people

  • Do not collect, publish or sell other people's personal information.
  • Do not record or share private conversations without permission.
  • Do not link anonymous activity to a real person in a way that exposes them.
  • Respect requests from people who ask you to remove personal information about them.

14. Protecting younger members

DDS is for people who are 16 and over. We take the safety of young people very seriously.

  • Do not ask for, collect or share personal details of anyone who appears to be under 18.
  • Do not attempt to contact young people for any sexual, romantic or inappropriate purpose.
  • Do not share content that is harmful to young people.
  • If you believe that someone is a child or is being targeted, report it to us immediately.

15. Reporting a violation

If you see something that breaks this policy, please tell us. You can use the report options inside DDS, the Contact button or the feedback form, or you can email darkdigitalspaceteam@gmail.com. Give us as much detail as you can, such as the alias, the link or the drop, the time and why you think it breaks the rules. Reports are handled in confidence as far as possible. Do not make false or malicious reports. Doing so is itself a violation.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

16. How we enforce this policy

16.1 How we find problems

We learn about problems from community reports, from our team and moderators, and from automated checks, which may scan text for prohibited phrases and look for patterns such as many accounts on one device or repeated abuse. Automated tools are only a first step. We try to have a person check serious cases.

16.2 What we may do

Our response depends on how serious the violation is, whether it was deliberate, the harm it caused and your history on DDS. We may:

  • remove or hide Content;
  • send a warning;
  • limit features, such as posting, replying, boosting or withdrawing;
  • adjust, freeze or remove bits, ranks and rewards, and cancel pending withdrawals;
  • suspend your account for a period of time;
  • close your account permanently;
  • ban a device, so that new accounts cannot be created from it; and
  • report the matter to the police or other authorities, and give them the information that we are required or allowed to give.

16.3 Severity levels

We rank problems from one to five. Level five is the most serious, such as threats, terrorism, hate speech, self-harm encouragement, trafficking and sexual content involving minors, and usually leads to an immediate permanent ban and urgent human review. Levels four and three include doxxing, extortion, weapons, drugs, malware, harassment, scams and fraud, and lead to strong penalties, which grow with repeated violations. Levels two and one cover impersonation, spam, advertising and cheating, which usually begin with removal and warnings. Repeat violations move you up the ladder.

16.4 No obligation to warn

We try to be fair and to explain our actions, but we do not have to warn you first, especially for serious violations or where there is a risk to people or to DDS.

17. Appeals

If you think that we made a mistake, you can appeal. Use the appeal option shown on your account screen if your account is banned, or email us at darkdigitalspaceteam@gmail.com with your DDS Number, the date and a clear explanation. A person who was not involved in the original decision will review it where possible, and we will tell you the result. We may keep a decision if we are satisfied that the rules were broken. Abusing the appeal system, for example by sending many false appeals, can lead to further action.

18. Working with the authorities

We may cooperate with the police, courts and regulators where the law requires or allows, and where it is necessary to protect people from serious harm. That may include sharing information about accounts, devices and activity, even where the content was posted anonymously.

19. Changes to this policy

We may update this Usage Policy as DDS grows and as new risks appear. Every edition has a version number and a date. At the top of this page you can see the current update and the previous update, and at the bottom you can read the full version history. For important changes, we will tell you through DDS or by email where we reasonably can. If you keep using DDS after a change, you accept the updated policy.

  • Email: darkdigitalspaceteam@gmail.com
  • In the app or on the site: use the Contact button or the feedback form
